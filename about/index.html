About

I’m Sarbhanu — engineer turned statistician. Currently studying M.Tech at the Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata.

I hold a Bachelor’s in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering. Signals, systems, noise — how information moves and where it breaks. At some point I realized probability distributions were telling the same story, just more honestly. One thing led to another.

I can’t leave a broken system alone — in a terminal, in a proof or in a conversation with someone who has never heard of either. I write Python mostly. R when the statistics demands it. C++ when Python isn’t fast enough. SQL when the data is somewhere it shouldn’t be. I’ve compiled a kernel from source. I’ll probably do it again.

Outside of work — markets, history, philosophy, geopolitics. I connect dots across things that don’t usually talk to each other. I’m told this is either a strength or a problem depending on who you ask.

Shoot me an email if you want to talk.
